History of the Miniature Schnauzer

The Miniature Schnauzer originated in Germany during the late nineteenth century. Breeders developed the small-sized breed from larger Schnauzers to create a capable farm and companion dog.

Throughout its development, the breed became known for intelligence, alertness and adaptability. Miniature Schnauzers were used to control rodents on farms while remaining reliable family companions.

In Nordic countries, the breed gained popularity because of its compact size, weather-resistant coat and suitability for both urban and rural lifestyles. Many owners participate in obedience, agility and dog shows.

Around the world, Miniature Schnauzers are valued as companion dogs. Their distinctive beard and eyebrows make them one of the most recognizable dog breeds internationally.
